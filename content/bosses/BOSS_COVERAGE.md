# Boss coverage

Last audited: 2026-11-12 against the OSRS Wiki boss roster.

This inventory follows the project's current target: combat bosses other than raids and
minigames. Mage Arena I and II are included because they are explicitly in scope. Quest-only
encounters that have no repeatable post-quest boss are not part of this list.

## Implemented

- Corporeal Beast
- Barrows brothers and reward chest
- Abyssal Sire, Alchemical Hydra, Araxxor, Cerberus, Grotesque Guardians, Kraken, and
  Thermonuclear smoke devil
- Dagannoth Kings
- Deranged archaeologist
- Duke Sucellus, the Leviathan, the Whisperer, and Vardorvis
- General Graardor, Kree'arra, Commander Zilyana, and K'ril Tsutsaroth
- Giant Mole
- The Hueycoatl, Gemstone Crab, and the three Moons of Peril
- Kalphite Queen
- King Black Dragon
- Mage Arena I: all five Kolodion forms
- Mage Arena II: Justiciar Zachariah, Derwen, and Porazdir
- Modern instances: Amoxliatl, Branda the Fire Queen, Eldric the Ice King, Yama, and the Doom
  of Mokhaiotl
- Nex
- Obor, Bryophyta, the Mimic, Hespori, and Skotizo
- Vorkath, Phantom Muspah, the Nightmare, and Phosani's Nightmare
- Sarachnis
- Scurrius
- Wilderness: Revenant maledictus, Callisto, Artio, Venenatis, Spindel, Vet'ion, Calvar'ion,
  Scorpia, Chaos Elemental, Chaos Fanatic, and Crazy Archaeologist
- Zulrah

## Not yet implemented

### Instanced bosses

- Brutus and Demonic Brutus
- Maggot King
- Mad Angel

### Slayer bosses

- Shellbane gryphon

The bosses remaining above postdate cache 233.4 and have no NPC definitions in this project's
client cache. They cannot be rendered, spawned, or integration-tested without a cache/client
upgrade; substituting unrelated NPC IDs would create invisible or incorrect encounters. The
currently planned 238.1 migration is also too old for Maggot King and Mad Angel. Completing this
list requires a revision-240-or-newer cache, symbols, protocol library, and matching client.

## Deliberately outside the current scope

- Raids: Chambers of Xeric, Theatre of Blood, and Tombs of Amascut
- Minigame bosses: the Gauntlet, Fight Caves, Inferno, and Fortis Colosseum
- Skilling bosses and activities: Tempoross, Wintertodt, and Zalcano
