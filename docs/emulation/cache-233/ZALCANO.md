# Zalcano

The native Prifddinas mining platform leads to Rhiannon and the prison barrier after Song of the Elves. The public encounter shares rock state, falling debris, demonic symbols, a healing golem and the boss’s health across participating players. Movement can avoid the telegraphed falling rock. Red symbols damage players and drain run energy, while blue symbols increase the maximum damage of imbued tephra by 50%. Weakening the shield interrupts the golem and clears symbols. Recovering after 12–16 seconds restores the shield and destroys remaining tephra. Players who remain in the chamber can fight the next spawn. A successful mining strike awards 35.2 Mining XP. Ordinary combat damage is suppressed.
